Editorial Standards

Editorial policy and newsroom standards

Mpumalanga Local Time is committed to accurate, fair and transparent journalism. Every published story is reviewed by a human editor before publication.

Editorial principles

  • Accuracy
  • Originality
  • Transparency
  • Fairness
  • Accountability
  • Public interest

AI governance

Artificial intelligence may assist with grammar, formatting, summaries and SEO support, but it never publishes, approves or rewrites facts without human review.

Review workflow

Every article moves from draft to fact-checking, editor review and approval before publication. Editors may request changes, reject, schedule or feature a story.
