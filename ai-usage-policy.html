AI Usage & Disclosure

Responsible AI use policy

Artificial intelligence supports productivity, but it does not replace human editorial judgment. All published work remains under human editorial control.

Allowed uses

AI may assist with grammar, spelling, formatting, summaries, headline ideas, metadata generation, tag suggestions and article classification.

Not allowed

AI may not invent facts, invent quotes, invent interviews, fabricate sources, auto-publish stories or approve articles without human review.
