Cookie Policy

Clear information about the cookies used by Mpumalanga Local Time and your choices.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, may serve a similar purpose. They help us remember choices, keep the site secure, understand how it is used, and support relevant services.

Cookies we use

Necessary cookies

These cookies are required for core functions such as security, account sessions, consent choices, and form protection. They cannot be switched off through the preference panel.

Functional cookies

These cookies remember optional choices and improve features. They are used only when you enable Functional Cookies.

Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand aggregate traffic and content performance. They are disabled unless you grant Analytics consent.

Marketing cookies

Marketing cookies may support advertising measurement or personalisation. They are disabled unless you grant Marketing consent.

Your choices

Select Cookie Settings at any time to accept all optional cookies, reject them, or customise each optional category. Your choice is saved in your browser and can be changed later. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent access to ordinary news content.

POPIA and consent

Where cookies involve personal information, we process that information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), our Privacy Policy, and our Privacy Statement. We do not make access to general news content conditional on accepting optional cookies.

Third-party services

Some optional services may place or read cookies when enabled. Their processing is subject to their own notices and contractual safeguards. We do not sell personal information collected through cookies.

Contact

For questions about cookies or your privacy rights, contact our Information Officer at privacy@mplocaltime.co.za.
